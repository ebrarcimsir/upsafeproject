Welcome to UPSAFE+ Project

"Welcome to UPSAFE! We are an international project working to make digital school environments safer and more supportive for everyone. Today, young people face many online challenges like cyberbullying, mean comments, and fake AI-content. Too often, schools try to fix these problems alone, without a shared plan. UPSAFE brings school leaders, teachers, students, and parents together. Running from 2026 to 2028 across Türkiye, Greece, and Poland, our goal is simple: to stop digital risks early, support students better, and make online safety everyone's shared responsibility."

What is UPSAFE?

UPSAFE stands for "Whole-School Upskilling for Safe Online Environments". We are an international Erasmus+ project running for 24 months, from October 2026 to September 2028. Our main goal is to help upper-secondary schools stop cyberbullying and build a safer digital space for everyone.

The Challenge: Why Do We Need This?

Today, young people face many serious risks online, such as:Cyberbullying and online exclusion Hate speech and online harassment Misleading or manipulative content created by artificial intelligence (AI) Usually, schools try to fix these problems one by one, without a shared plan. Because of this, it is hard to notice dangers early or support students effectively.

Our Solution: A Team Effort for the Whole School

Instead of leaving online safety to just one teacher or student, UPSAFE brings everyone together. Our "whole-school" approach includes: School Leaders & Teachers: To set up clear rules, spot risks early, and handle reports securely. Students: To learn safe online habits, digital literacy, and lead peer support activities. Parents: To build a strong bridge between home and school, and raise awareness. We also pay special attention to disadvantaged learners, making sure all students get the protection and support they need.

Project Facts

Programme: Erasmus+ Cooperation Partnerships in School Education (KA220-SCH) Duration: 24 Months (01 October 2026 – 30 September 2028) Total Budget: €120,000 Pilot Reach: Testing our model in 40 schools (30 in Türkiye, 5 in Greece, and 5 in Poland)

Pilot Footprint (40 Target Schools)

To make sure our tools work in real life, UPSAFE will test its whole-school model directly in 40 upper-secondary schools: Türkiye: 30 Pilot Schools Greece: 5 Pilot Schools Poland: 5 Pilot Schools Field feedback from these schools will help us improve and finalize the toolkit so it can be easily used by any school in Europe.

Work Packages

To reach our goals, the UPSAFE project is organized into 5 strategic Work Packages (WPs) over 24 months, supported by a total budget of €120,000. Each work package focuses on a specific part of the project to ensure everything runs smoothly, from research to real-school testing.

WP1: Project Management & Quality Assurance

Lead Partner: ŞAB (Türkiye) Budget: €18,000 What we do: Keeps the project organized, manages the overall budget, ensures high quality, and coordinates all activities between partners.

WP2: Needs Analysis & Baseline Assessment

Lead Partner: Uniwersytet VIZJA (Poland) Budget: €22,000 What we do: Conducts surveys and focus groups across Türkiye, Greece, and Poland to understand the exact digital risks students face and what schools need most.

WP3: Whole-School Model & Toolkit Development

Lead Partner: Tuzla BİLSEM (Türkiye) Budget: €24,000 What we do: Builds practical tools for schools, including incident reporting protocols, teacher guides, student peer ambassador training, and parent awareness content.

WP4: Pilot Implementation & Learning Mobility

Lead Partner: ŞAB & Consortium Budget: €31,500 (Largest share of budget) What we do: Tests our toolkit in 40 real schools (30 TR, 5 GR, 5 PL) and hosts a 4-day international learning mobility event to gather direct field feedback.

WP5: Dissemination & Sustainability

Co-Leads: IAKE (Greece) & YEYODER (Türkiye) Budget: €24,500 What we do: Shares our results with the world through a 1,000+ entry open digital hub, national webinars, scientific articles, and community outreach.
