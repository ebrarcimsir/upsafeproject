Welcome to UPSAFE+ Project

Welcome to UPSAFE! We are an international project working to make digital school environments safer and more supportive for everyone. Today, young people face many online challenges like cyberbullying, mean comments, and fake AI-content. Too often, schools try to fix these problems alone, without a shared plan. UPSAFE brings school leaders, teachers, students, and parents together. Running from 2026 to 2028 across Türkiye, Greece, and Poland, our goal is simple: to stop digital risks early, support students better, and make online safety everyone's shared responsibility.

What is UPSAFE?

UPSAFE stands for "Whole-School Upskilling for Safe Online Environments". We are an international Erasmus+ project running for 24 months, from October 2026 to September 2028. Our main goal is to help upper-secondary schools stop cyberbullying and build a safer digital space for everyone.

The Challenge: Why Do We Need This?

Today, young people face many serious risks online, such as cyberbullying and online exclusion, hate speech and online harassment, and misleading or manipulative content created by artificial intelligence (AI). Usually, schools try to fix these problems one by one, without a shared plan. Because of this, it is hard to notice dangers early or support students effectively.

Our Solution: A Team Effort for the Whole School

Instead of leaving online safety to just one teacher or student, UPSAFE brings everyone together. Our "whole-school" approach includes school leaders and teachers, who set clear rules, spot risks early, and handle reports securely; students, who learn safe online habits, digital literacy, and peer support; and parents, who build a strong bridge between home and school and raise awareness. We also pay special attention to disadvantaged learners, making sure all students get the protection and support they need.

Project Facts

Programme: Erasmus+ Cooperation Partnerships in School Education (KA220-SCH) | Duration: 24 months (1 October 2026 – 30 September 2028) | Total Budget: €120,000 | Pilot Reach: 40 schools (30 in Türkiye, 5 in Greece, and 5 in Poland).

Pilot Footprint (40 Target Schools)

To make sure our tools work in real life, UPSAFE will test its whole-school model directly in 40 upper-secondary schools: 30 in Türkiye, 5 in Greece, and 5 in Poland.

Work Packages

To reach our goals, the UPSAFE project is organized into five strategic Work Packages (WPs) over 24 months, supported by a total budget of €120,000.

WP1: Project Management & Quality Assurance

Lead Partner: ŞAB (Türkiye) | Budget: €18,000 | What we do: Keeps the project organized, manages the overall budget, ensures high quality, and coordinates all activities between partners.

WP2: Needs Analysis & Baseline Assessment

Lead Partner: Uniwersytet VIZJA (Poland) | Budget: €22,000 | What we do: Conducts surveys and focus groups across Türkiye, Greece, and Poland to understand exact digital risks.

WP3: Whole-School Model & Toolkit Development

Lead Partner: Tuzla BİLSEM (Türkiye) | Budget: €24,000 | What we do: Builds practical tools for schools, including incident reporting protocols and teacher guides.

WP4: Pilot Implementation & Learning Mobility

Lead Partner: ŞAB & Consortium | Budget: €31,500 | What we do: Tests our toolkit in 40 real schools and hosts a four-day international learning mobility event.

WP5: Dissemination & Sustainability

Co-Leads: IAKE (Greece) & YEYODER (Türkiye) | Budget: €24,500 | What we do: Shares our results through an open digital hub, national webinars, and scientific articles.
